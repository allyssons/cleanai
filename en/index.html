WHAT CLEANING DOES

Know what each tool changes

Clean image metadata

Photos may include EXIF, GPS, camera details, editing history, and content credentials. The tool re-exports JPEG, PNG, or WebP from its pixels, leaving out compatible metadata it does not preserve. JPEG files are recompressed.

This removes signals stored in metadata, not watermarks embedded in pixels. It cannot guarantee how a social network will classify a post.

Image metadata guide →

Clean invisible text characters

Copying between apps can add special spaces, zero-width marks, and invisible controls. The tool shows common groups it finds and cleans selected marks while preserving joiners needed for languages and emoji.

It does not identify who wrote text, rewrite words, or remove statistical signals based on word choice.

Invisible text guide →

Clean common video metadata

MP4 and MOV files can contain title, date, editing software, and location fields. The tool removes recognized types without re-encoding audio or video and leaves the original intact.

Recognizable provenance credentials are not changed; watermarks embedded in video frames remain in the content.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you
share.

What cleanup can do, and where its limits are.

Does this remove AI labels or watermarks?

No. There is no guarantee that a platform will change a label. The tools do not remove watermarks embedded in pixels or video frames. The video tool stops if it finds a recognizable C2PA indicator.

Does the text tool make writing sound human?

No. It removes common invisible Unicode characters and normalizes special spaces. It does not rewrite words or remove statistical signals based on word choice.

Are my files uploaded or stored?

No. Reading and cleaning happen in your browser. AI Content Cleaner does not receive or store the images, videos, documents, or text you select.

Does it work with iPhone HEIC photos?

Not yet. Export the photo as JPEG on your iPhone and try again. The image tool accepts JPEG, PNG, and WebP.

Which video formats are supported?

The video tool accepts MP4 and MOV files up to 200 MB. It cleans only common fields it recognizes; unknown blocks may remain and some files are rejected for safety.

Can I clean very large images?

Images over 40 megapixels or 40 MB are rejected to reduce the chance of running out of memory on a phone or computer.
