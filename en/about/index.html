ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Simple cleanup, with clear limits.

AI Content Cleaner brings together free tools to remove common image and video metadata and clean invisible characters from text. Each tool helps you review a copy before sharing.

What you can do here

For images, choose JPEG, PNG, or WebP. Your browser re-exports the pixels to create a new copy without compatible metadata the process does not preserve, such as EXIF and GPS information. The original file is not changed.

For text, paste content or open a .txt or .md file. You can analyze common invisible Unicode characters and special spaces, review the results, and clean selected marks. Your words are not rewritten.

You can also clean common fields in MP4 and MOV videos. PDF, Office document, and tracked-link tools are currently available in Portuguese.

How processing works

Reading and cleaning happen in your device’s browser. Images, videos, and text are not sent to the AI Content Cleaner server for processing. Copies and previews may remain in browser memory while you use the page.

The site may load separate measurement and advertising services based on your choices and provider settings. These services do not receive the content you select in the tools. See the privacy policy for details about optional analytics, advertising, and preferences.

What cleanup does not promise

Image cleanup does not remove watermarks embedded in pixels, determine whether an image was created with AI, or guarantee that a platform will remove a label. Re-exporting may slightly change image quality, color, or size.

Text cleanup does not identify authorship, make content sound human, or remove statistical signals based on word choice. Some invisible characters are needed for languages or emoji and are preserved by default.

Video cleanup covers only recognized fields in MP4/MOV. Recognizable C2PA credentials make the tool stop; it does not validate credentials or guarantee recognition of every provenance format. The tools do not remove watermarks from video frames.

Always review the result before using or publishing it. You remain responsible for your content, its associated rights, and following the rules of the services where you share it. Read the terms of use.

A free service

AI Content Cleaner is offered for free and may show advertising when permitted and configured by the providers. Ad availability depends on platform approval and account settings. Tools may change over time; each tool page describes the limits of the available version.
