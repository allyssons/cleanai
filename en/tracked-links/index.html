TRACKED LINK CLEANER

Clean tracked links

Remove recognized tracking parameters from one or more URLs while preserving the rest of each address. Everything happens in your browser.

Clean links on this device

One URL per line · up to 120,000 characters · original text stays in your browser

◈ Processed on your device

iKnown trackers only: UTM and selected advertising or analytics parameters are removed. Unknown parameters are preserved because they may be needed by the destination website.

What gets removed from a link

The cleaner recognizes common UTM parameters and identifiers such as fbclid, gclid, dclid, msclkid, wbraid, gbraid, and selected analytics identifiers. It removes only those known query parameters. The page path, other query parameters, and URL fragment are preserved.

Review the result before sharing

Unknown parameters are left in place because a website may need them for search, language, product selection, or other functions. Invalid URLs, non-HTTP addresses, and links containing credentials stay unchanged and are flagged for review. The tool does not connect to the destination website or guarantee that every tracking method has been removed.

How to use it

  1. Paste one URL per line or load the example.
  2. Choose “Clean known trackers”.
  3. Review the output and any warnings, then copy the result.

Text remains in your browser. For document cleanup, see PDF metadata and Office metadata.
